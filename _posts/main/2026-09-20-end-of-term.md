Friday was my last day of school, so now I'm officially on holiday from being on holiday. It feels good to be done with school, even though I've still got a long way to go with my Japanese. I'm looking forward to a more relaxing few weeks before I come home.

Got a friend coming over next week so I'll get ready for that, make sure I know what we're doing to make the most of our time. Looking forward to it.

Been doing more of the usual recently, arcade, chess, pokemon and cube. Arcade is probably where I'm seeing the most progress, probably because it's the thing I am newest to. My housemates have said my chess has gotten better though, so that's nice. I watched a few youtube videos on chess fundamentals which helps me with the early game, so I'm not wasting too much time mulling about. 

Last week was exams week at school. Wednesday were the main written and listening tests, but that was the one day all term that my train hit delays, so I was about 45 minutes late. Luckily they waited for people to arrive before starting, which the other students were very unsympathetic towards. I think a lot of them just want to be done with school as quickly as possible and go out and play. I did alright in the exams themselves, maybe I was a little flustered to start off with, which lost me 2  marks, but I got most of everything else right. My conversation test was good too, didn't make any mistakes there. I'm feeling like I'm slowly piecing things together, but when I look at some of the people from the intermediate class, I wonder how I'm ever going to reach their level. On one hand it's daunting, but on the other they show it's possible to get there.

A new ruleset came out in pokemon so it's been fun playing around with that. I haven't put loads of time into it though, especially last week with exams I was mainly focussing on studying. Maybe this week I'll play a bit more. 

With sound voltex I'm starting level 12 songs, feels like I made some progress pretty quickly over the last week or two. I'm definitely finding the game gets more fun as you get better.

Honestly not too much else going on. Watched the odessey as an end of term celebration thing with some friends. It was good. There's a funny story about the martian, a film with Matt Damon trying to get home. In Japan it's called オデッセイ (odyssey). Then the odyssey came out, which is called オデュッセイア (Odyssia). It's also a film with Matt Damon trying to get home. Must be very confusing for the Japanese people.

In case anyone's wondering, I'm not planning much for the rest of my time here. I have enough time to do something cool like go travelling around again, but I don't really have much interest in doing so. I'll go to Nara and then Osaka to meet my friend, and maybe I'll do the odd day trip here and there, but I don't have that much appetite for adventure right now. I've quite enjoyed exploring random towns in Tokyo, it's pretty easy to just take the train to a new place, then walk around the immediate surroundings. Japan seems to be pretty good at organising neighbourhoods around train stations, so it's a pretty good way of exploring.

Bit of a shorter one this week, even though I've felt pretty busy. I've just been studying and playing games, and there's not much to write home about with those. But yeah, looking forward to a few days of chilling out and planning out the rest of my time.
