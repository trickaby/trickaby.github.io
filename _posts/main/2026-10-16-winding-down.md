Since I got back from Kochi, I haven't been up to that much really. At least it doesn't feel like it. I had quite a few days of just relaxing, I was exhausted from my travels and being a tour guide. Started getting back into my gym routine now that I've finally got absolutely no excuses left. It's usually a late morning thing, then I'll maybe do something in the afternoon or evening. 

Been to lunch with school friends a few times a week, they're back for a new term. It's definitely starting to feel like things are winding down, I think I'm about ready to go back. Still feel like I've got too many things I'd like to do before I go, but they're all DLC stuff at this point. I guess maybe the one main thing I haven't done yet is a proper hike. I hear Takao san is the most popular place for a day trip from Tokyo, so I'll try and do that one day.

Also been hanging out a bit more with housemates, one of them moved out the other day, so wanted to do loads of adventures before leaving. I'm happy just being a "go-along" person, so that filled out a few days.

Got a list of things to do with my last few weeks, I doubt I'll clear it though. The days go pretty quickly, especially because my sleep schedule is pretty bad at the moment. I'll try and get to sleep earlier and have a more consistent wake up time, then hopefully I can be more productive. One thing on the list is the Sumida street jazz festival, which is coming up this weekend. Looking forward to it.

I had a minor adventure with work, who said I needed a criminal record certificate for me to get my security clearance. It meant me taking a trip to the big police office. I was pretty stressed about it because they need evidence, which I had to go and print off at a conbini, and I was just generally worried about the procedure and how smoothly it would go for a non-Japanese person. I was also worried about the 2 week waiting period, which was cutting it fine with my flight home.

Thankfully it all went very smoothly, I didn't have to wait at all, and everyone at the office was very friendly. I gave over my documents, filled out a form, got my fingerprints taken and was sent on my way, with a little card telling me to pick up my certificate on the 23rd, comfortably before my flight.

Now that I'm on holiday, I'm also spending a lot more money. It's nothing severe, but just noticeable now that I've got more time, I've got more opportunity to give money away. In that sense I am somehow looking forward to getting back to the day to day boring routine of work.

Hobbies update, haven't been playing as much sound voltex recently, after having played loads for a few weeks. I'm catching up to some of my friends, around level 12/13 at the moment. I imagine I won't progress much more now. It's been a fun little distraction for the last couple of months. I think I'm getting worse at chess, haven't played much of that recently either. Also haven't practiced my cubing so that's probably gotten worse too. I've got a new pokemon team in champions, it started out as a hard trick room team with indeedee F and armarouge setting up terrain and TR, with torkoal and mega crab in the back. The idea is to follow me with indeedee, hopefully sack it to bring in torkoal who can sweep in the sun. Crab is difficult to use though for me, so I swapped it out for some more flexibility, I've now got a more standard mega raichu/raptor duo, along with milotic to help with some of my bad matchups against dragon and fire. Raichu is good because it helps against archaludon, who is public enemy number one at the moment. Torkoal is my counter to rain teams but they're still a bit of a problem for me. I haven't played with the staraptor yet, that last slot has been a bit up in the air. Used metagross for a bit for coverage, it was only ok.

When I get home I'm looking forward to changing up my study strategy for Japanese. I'll continue plugging away at kanji, but I might try to add more native immersion, things like watching anime, playing games, reading books etc. I probably have to learn more grammar too though, perhaps just grabbing a textbook is best. I've still got a pokemon game in Japanese to get through, and I've also started watching a stream of pokemon green in Japanese, so maybe it isn't a bad idea to gradually go through all the games so I build up a knowledge of the names of moves, pokes, etc. A little sub goal of mine is to learn all the Japanese names of pokemon, there's about 1000 at the moment, and hopefully a decent enough chunk of the names are the same as in Englsh.

Weirdly, I haven't watched any anime in ages. Probably since I moved into my new house. No particular reason, just haven't been that bothered. Still plodding my way through naruto, maybe one day I'll finish it. It's also dawning on me that I'm not really much of a TV person, even in English, so I probably won't get around to watching a lot of what's out there.

Weather's getting cooler, still nice and warm. Haven't worn more than a t-shirt since spring and I probably won't before I go back home. Sort of dreading go back to colder weather.
