Another interesting few weeks, but not necessarily for the right reasons. I picked up a bit of a throaty cold that kept me away from school for most of the last two weeks. Last week was midterm week and I missed all the exams for it. The cold sounds worse than it was though, I just decided that since I'm on holiday and this school is entirely optional for me, I wouldn't push myself into going. Getting up and out of the door in time was stressing me out too, so it maed sense to just stay home until I felt better. I'm still doing fine with school itself, still understanding pretty much everything. I think we learned some new grammars that I need to catch up on though. I'm also pretty behind on homework. I've got enough time though so not too worried. 

I probably didn't do myself a load of favours when I was sick, I just rotted in bed a bit too much. I also wasn't eating super great just because I could never be bothered to go to the shops or plan out my meals properly. 

Anyway, feeling alright now, still got a bit of a lingering cough but otherwise I'm good. Would be nice to go back to the gym soon though, I'm not really getting my money's worth at the moment.

I've been a bit better at getting to know my sharehouse mates recently too. Unfortunately the trick seems to be hanging out in the living room late at night, when I'd rather be asleep. Had a nice long chat with a Japanese guy about his holiday to the UK, but it meant I didn't get to sleep until 1:30. Oh well, I didn't have school the next day so it's fine.

It's been interesting learning a bit about Japanese work culture too. It was stuff I had heard about in the past but good to hear from real working Japanese people. One guy sleeps from 2-8 most days, which I find ridiculous. He tends to get home pretty late too, not having dinner until maybe 11 or midnight. When I told my Japanese housemates about my work pattern they were rather shocked. They also said a lot of people do unpaid overtime, and you basically can't go home until you've finished your work for that day. Sounds pretty tough, don't think I'm cut out for it.

It's a side quest of mine at the moment to just try and get to know more about ordinary Japanese life, things like what they eat on a normal day, work life balance etc. So far it seems pretty consistent with what I've previously heard. 

Went to Enoshima yesterday with a housemate. It was pretty spontaneous, I saw him in the kitchen in the morning and he asked if I wanted to come. I had loads of jobs like laundry and study that day, but I decided to leave them half done and head out together. 

Enoshima is a little island thingy near Kamakura, maybe an hour and a half journey from Tokyo. It has really nice views, including of Fuji. Probably more likely to see it in winter/spring time though, summer tends to be hazy. I think I caught sight of it once while we were walking along.

Also had a nice lunch with views out to the sea. They had an option for terraced seating or anywhere seating, I was leaning towards anywhere seating because it would be less of a wait. My friend wanted to go terraced though, and that turned out to be the right decision. We agreed it would have been better if there was a canopy or something, cos we were in direct sunlight the whole time. It was pretty hot, around 30 that day.

I had a whole grilled squid, which was sliced up and served teishoku style. Was pretty good but a bit chewy for me. My friend had sashimi which looked really good.

Afterwards we went to a cave which was tourism-flavour. It was decent but honestly pretty small for the effort it took to get there. I wasn't complaining though, it was nice just to walk around and see the island.

Then we went back to the beach to walk along the promenade. If I was more prepared I might have brouhgt my swimming stuff, but I wasn't so I didn't. Still it was nice to just sit near the sea and hear the waves while we chatted a bit. At around half 5 loads of surfer people started congregating on the beach, but not going in the water. then maybe 15 minutes later they all went in to start surfing. There must have been some kind of curfew they were waiting for. We watched the surfers for about 10 minutes before heading home for the day. All in all it was a nice day.

Back in Nihombashi we stopped for abura soba, which I only discovered recently. It's really good though. Probably not the healthiest but who cares. Mum isn't here to be strict about my diet...

Today I've got futsal, then a house party in the evening. Seems like with the sharehouse I'm gradually getting a bit more sociable which is nice. Got someone else moving in today so that'll be good.

Also planning to visit a pokemon centre with a school friend in a few days. Oh forgot to mention it's summer holiday at the moment, so I get a whole 3 days off! Lucky me. I've been watching a lot of pokemon streams recently, especially while I was sick. That and Frasier. One of my housemates is from Seatle but he's never even heard of the show. Young people these days, honestly.
