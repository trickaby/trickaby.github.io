Before I forget what I did over the weekend here's a little bonus episode. 

Been playing a bit more chess with a housemate and I'm getting routinely trounced. I think I'm getting a bit better though, but I probably need a weaker opponent at the moment. 

Cubing a bit too, trying to learn some faster algorithms, but I'm not putting loads of time into it. 

Went to Koenji on Saturday and just walked around. They've got a lot of second hand clothes shops, and cafes and the like. It's a bit quieter than a lot of Tokyo which I enjoyed. I didn't have any particular plans there other than to just walk around and see what's what. I think it's that sort of place.

Didn't manage to get to akihabara though, Sunday was a washout. We played a game thing that my housemate organised, you had to do challenges around town and first one to finish wins. It was pretty fun.

Monday there was a typhoon apparently so school was online. It's not as good as in person but you get a bit more time in the day. I did nothing with said time.

Tuesday was presentation day, we went to an event hall in yoyogi and some students from the different classes. A lot of the students didn't come because it was a full day thing, by the end of it I was the only person there from my class. In fairness a lot of people came but had to leave for work.

I really enjoyed it though! It's nice seeing students that have put in the work getting their moment, and some of the presentations were really fun. Some did little acting pieces, or voiceover of anime, or short stories. The whole day was in Japanese (perhaps obviously), which is good listening practice.

Hasn't played any sound voltex for about a week so I was getting withdrawals. Did a long stint today though, got up to level 10. It's starting to get a bit fast and chaotic but I'm getting better so I can just about keep up.

Tonight is another game event thingy, I don't really know what I should call them. Anyway, should be fun. School is also nearly finished, which is a bit sad. Got the last few days of lessons then next week is final exam week.  Did my last couple of homework sheets this afternoon too, which was one of the first signs of this trip starting to come to an end. I haven't really decided what to do with the rest of it, but I've got quite a few activities keeping me occupied. I should probably plan a few trips away from Tokyo at some point. Maybe just day trips is enough though.
