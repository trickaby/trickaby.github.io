The last week and a bit have been weird to say the least. I think I wrote my last post when I was on the way to Tottori. It's a small town kind of near Hiroshima but not really. It's hot there, by the beach and apparently they have sand dunes.

I say apparently because I never made it there. Something bad must have been going on in my head, because at the hotel I decided to have a big panic attack. I'm fine now, but it was pretty bad at the time. It was probably just an accumulation of a lot of stress over the day, and probably over a few weeks in general. And maybe just being a bit overwhelmed by a lot of newness and uncertainty.

Anyway, my friends decided it would be best to do our own thing in Tottori, so I walked around for a bit in the heat. I was struggling to eat enough so that was my main objective, more than seeing the sights. After a bit I got too hot and bothered, so sought refuge in a maccies. I had 5 chicken nuggets and a coke which took me about an hour and a half to finish. A local came up to me and had a chat (in English, sorry to disappoint). He was pretty friendly and it was nice to have someone to talk to for a bit.

After that I just wanted to get to Matsue, which was my next stop. This was where we had planned to part ways anyway, as my friends were headed to Hiroshima. I took a bus to the train station, bought my ticket and waited for the train to arrive. The journey was nice, Japanese countryside is really pleasant and very different to most other countries I've been to.

Matsue itself also seemed really cool. But again I didn't really see it. I had 2 nights there, so evening and one full day, then an early flight home. I was pretty much in survival mode the whole weekend though, so it was just me venturing out to a conbini and getting some food I thought I could stomach, then retiring back to the hotel and spending ages trying to eat it. That and studying. I managed to eat all my food and got to bed early. Took the bus to the airport the next morning, while I was waiting for it to arrive I caught up on the world cup final, which was rubbish by the looks of it.

The week since has been a bit weird, my friend at school doesn't seem to want to talk to anyone. I think something's just going on, so I need to give a bit of space. Hopefully things are better soon though cos it's pretty uncomfortable.

Slight tangent, but a few people have asked me since - if I'm not eating or drinking, and if I'm quieter than usual (can be hard to tell I know), then maybe it means I'm not doing so good. Also my face is usually a dead giveaway. 

Friday was a school trip to Fuji, and one of my friends from last term, who didn't sign up for a second, came to join us. It was really nice to see him and catch up on his adventures since I'd last seen him. Most of the time is spent just sitting on the bus so we had loads of time to chat in depth about things. We also just happened to talk about interesting things that day, which made it a good one. Afterwards we went for Thai food which, in my opinion, was pretty average. Oh well eh, can't win them all.

Yesterday I went to a festival with housemates. It was the Sumida river fireworks festival. It was pretty good but to be honest I'm not a big fireworks guy. It was nice seeing everyone out and about though, loads of people lining the river, and being marshalled along by policepeople. 

We also did 大掃除 which is a "big cleaning" of the sharehouse. Entailed a lot of emptying out fridges, cupboard, storage spaces etc and throwing loads of stuff away. I actually really enjoyed it. It was nice just hanging out with people and working towards a goal. And satisfying to make our house a lot tidier. I wasn't even bothered by the fact that exactly 0 of the waste was mine and I was doing all this work to get rid of it. My favourite part was cleaning the tupperware shelf, and pairing all the containers with their lids. It was very satisfying, like doing a really boring jigsaw puzzle.

Some former tenant left some gyoza in the freezer, so our reward was to eat that once we'd finished cleaning. It was good.

Was meant to play football today but the gods have intervened. That's two weekends running without football! Starting to get worrying...

I did wonder whether to include all this in this weeks blog, but in the end I decided it would be best. Too much of the world these days filters out the bad stuff and leaves us with a warped perception of the world. I mean I'm still doing it to a certain extent, I mainly just talk about the highlights of my week, or the most interesting things that happened. I don't really talk about the times when I went to hamazushi and ate chips, or the 10 kanji I learned that week. 

Another reason is that I'm way more comfortable writing about these things than I am talking about them. Also saves me having to tell the same story over to different people. Usually I'd keep this to myself but here I've been telling pretty much everyone who speaks good enough English to understand. It's like the most interesting thing that's happened recently so I might as well!

I'm also increasingly grateful for some of these lowlights in life, as well as the highlights. I think we learn a lot from them, I certainly have over the last week. They can also give us a bit of perspective. For me, in this case it showed that I'm pushing myself out of my comfort zone. On this occasion maybe a bit far, but overall I think it's good.

Anyway, I hope everyone's doing well, and can enjoy the 20 minutes of peace while there's no football on. I'm back in London end of October
