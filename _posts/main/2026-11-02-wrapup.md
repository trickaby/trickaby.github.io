I'm back home now, I assume most people are aware, but I might as well wrap things up now that my Japan adventure is over.

Flight back was good, got a bit of sleep which was a surprise. Getting home definitely felt weird, everything at home had a sense of unfamiliarity, small things like the dials on the stove and my work desk. That wore off pretty quickly though and now everything feels normal.

My last few days in Japan were fairly quiet, I was getting over a cold so didn't go crazy. Did a bit of last minute shopping, and meeting up with friends for the last time. I do hope to be back in Japan one day to meet some of the people that are still there.

Highlight of Japan was probably travelling around with mum in March. Looking back, it's crazy how much we managed to do, how much of Japan we got to see. I'm also really glad I spent the time to join language school too. I think I got better at Japanese, it's always hard for me to see my progress though. My plan is to take it easy for now and maybe go back to proper study in the new year.

The things I've been looking forward to most about coming back has really just been catching up with friends and family. It's been really nice to see people again after such a long time, and it quickly feels as though I've never left. There's also a new member of the family I got to finally meet, which has probably been the most exciting thing. Aside from that, roasts, English desserts, and mum's Indian food.

I'll miss a lot from Japan, obviously sushi is a big one. I want to try sushi here again, just to compare it with what my new standard is. I'm expecting it to be nowhere near as good, but now that I've had Japanese sushi it'll be interesting to see how it compares. Also conbini. Can't believe I can't just walk out for some fried chicken at the drop of a hat anymore. The Japanese people as well just make it such a nice country to live in. Everyone is so kind and friendly, you feel very safe and relaxed.

Another thing is the water. As in the type you shower with. I've only been back a few days but already the London hard water is making my face feel all papery and my hair feel like straw. I suppose the sudden (for me) cold weather isn't helping either. It's just another thing you take for granted until you don't have it.

Overall though I'm glad to be back. I loved my time in Japan, but it's also time to come home. I'm looking forward to getting back to some new challenges at work, as well as finally earning some money again. I'll be back though, definitely for holidays. One thing I wanted to figure out was whether I'd want to live in Japan long term. I'm still not 100% sure, but I think Japan is probably the only foreign country I'd consider now. Partly because I've put in so much effort into learning the language, it'd feel pretty silly to instead move to, say, Korea. It's tough being so far from home, I definitely had times when I felt homesick. That being said, I'll probably now have times when I feel Japansick. Can't win I suppose. One issue with Japan is that the wages just aren't very good. One of the reasons I was able to enjoy myself so much and for so long, was because my pounds went a long way. Earning yen feels a lot less enticing. If someone came knocking with a good salary, it'd probably be something I consider. The language would still be a difficulty, at least for a while. Even though I've improved a lot, it's still nothing like speaking in your native language.

For now though, I'm happy to be back home.
