I'm just starting to think these should maybe be part of an autumn adventures thing or something. I'll keep them daily, and date them to the day they happened, but I'm writing them all in advance.
For now I'll just keep them as regular posts, maybe reformat later.

Anyway, we went back to akiba for the afternoon, did that kind of shopping where you don't actually buy anything, played guess what at the game centre, then had some curry at gogo curry. I hadn't been there before so wanted to try, it was good but somehow I managed to mess up my order and didn't get any meat. Oh well, better luck next time.

I was kinda worn out so headed home while my friend got settled into his room. I managed to convince him to come to Ningyocho for dinner, which is where I live. We went to Jonathan's for dinner, which is kinda like an American family restaurant, I'd call it Japanese western food. They have a soupy spaghetti dish that I had last time, and idk it's weirdly good. I got it again and we shared something else, I think it was from the seasonal menu. I remember there were fried oysters, chicken wings, oh and a rice and fish bowl. That's what it was.

See this is why I need to write these posts earlier, I forget lots. I've just been too busy.

I've resolved to get everything up to date this evening now. It's already 11 so this is probably a bad idea. I'm eating an emergency dinner which is leftover salad and chicken from before Osaka. It's... not great.

Anyway, after the main we got some things from the seasonal dessert menu. It was chocomint themed. I got a cake and my friend got a parfait. They're pretty common in Japan. I guess it's just what we'd call a sundae? These tend to have jelly in them though.

After dinner we walked to Ginza, which I quite like at night. It's still got all the nice lights and expensive buildings, but all the annoying humans have gone home because the shops are closed. The shops being closed is of no consequence to us, obviously.

After ginza we headed home. Another long day but another good one.

I'm going to carry on powering through this salad and these posts. Now it's ten past.
