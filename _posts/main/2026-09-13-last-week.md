This week was the last week of classes proper, next week is exams week. It feels pretty big getting to the end, I'm glad I did it.

This week I've been doing the normal, going class, having lunch after, then playing in the arcade. Went to the gym a few times too. 

New rules of pokemon champions came out too, so I've been playing that. I'm trying out a trick room team, it's decent but I don't have great options yet for trick room setters. It's still fun though. Currently playing mega metagross and mega crab, meta is more solid but crab is more fun. Sinistcha is my trick room setter but sometimes it just dies which isn't great. Would be nice to get a farig or indeedee.

I'm slowly learning some algorithms for the rubiks cube to get solving a bit quicker. It's kind of a back seat hobbie at the moment though so I'm not making progress very quickly. Sometimes I'll just fiddle with it while watching tv or something.

Chess is going well, I got another win against my housemate, both times I've beaten him have been on time, I don't think I've actually checkmated him yet, and he plays with a lot less time than me. But I'll take it as progress. To give you an idea of who I'm up against, this evening I came downstairs and saw 2 chess books he'd checked out of the library. I can't compete with someone who actually *reads*.

Went to Nakano today (Sunday), for ramen at ichiran. It's a chain where you get to eat without having to interact with the staff face to face. It's a cool idea, if a little strange. You'd think it would be up my alley, given I don't really like having to interact with people, but honestly you still get spoken to by people but you just can't see their faces. For whatever reason that doesn't help me in any way.

Either way the ramen was good, and the experience itself was novel and interesting. I would go back. It's pretty tourist friendly too with English language support and ways to nonverbally communicate any requests such as for extra noodles.

Also did another game thingy, this time was in pairs. Our team won, so now I've got 2 wins and a second place in 3 games. It's pretty fun, and it's nice to help out my friend who's trying to turn this idea in to a business. I am starting to get a bit tired of playing though, so maybe I'll skip a game or two so I don't get sick of it.

Also had a flat party on Friday, as one of our housemates is leaving and a former housemate is flying home. It was a bit of a letdown though, a lot of people didn't come and some got stuck at work until late. We ended up watching the last hour or so of titanic which was on TV. I've never seen the film start to finish but I've probably seen about 60% of the thing in snippets here and there. Probably seen enough of it to not be interested in ever seeing it from start to finish.

Once the party had finished and most people had gone home, a few of us played some chess, which was decent fun. We did doubles, one beginner and one experienced on each team. I blundered my queen to lose the first game, before dominating the next two. One of them was a stalemate though because my teammate didn't execute the ladder mate I'd set up, but that's ok. After that I played 1 on 1 with one of the beginners, playing with missing pieces for a bit of a training ground environment, gradually increasing the number of my pieces on the board as we went along. It was a pretty good session I think, I didn't really give much advice or information. I think these things are better to just figure out gradually by yourself. There's also so much the brain can take in at a time.

Anyway I didn't end up sleeping that night until 3, but Saturday was a quiet day with nothing planned, so I didn't mind. Just played loads of pokemon. Also went to a shopping street near Tokyo station that I never even knew existed. It was cool.

The Odyssey just came out in Japan so I might tag along with some friends if they go watch it. I've very much adopted the 'tag along-er' role since I've been here, it suits me nicely. Luckily there's enough stuff just kind of happening around me that I'm not really having to go look for stuff. Plus I've got enough single player hobbies to keep me going, as ever.

Also looking forward to my friend from back home coming to visit, and going back to Osaka will be fun. I haven't planned anything, hopefully I can just 'local' my way through it all. We don't have loads of time to fill anyway.

The weather appears to be settling into something a bit cooler, closer to English conditions. I've missed those cool overcast days where the weather is just there, it's not *doing* anything to you. Japan spring was better than English weather. I guess technically the summer was too, if you're comparing 1-1. But I've missed drizzle and cloud. I guess England has too...

It will be strange when school finishes, because then it's just the last sprint to the end, then I'll be back home. A month or so ago I was really looking forward to being back, but I must be enjoying myself more now because I wouldn't mind staying a bit longer.
