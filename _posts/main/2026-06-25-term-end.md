First term of school is done, exams all finished for now. Overall did pretty well at school, got a little digital certificate for being a good student. 

Last week and a half has been the term end exams, a meeting with teacher, and getting books for next term.

Pretty sad cos some of the guys are heading home. But there's still some people staying here which is nice. 

Going to move into my sharehouse next week. I'm looking forward to it but also a little apprehensive, never know exactly what it'll be like, what the people will be like. 

Glad to have a break from class. I think I need a bit of time to chill out and relax. The weather isn't great, which makes me less inclined to go out. I'll try and get out a few times though. Maybe some parks. When I was speaking to sensei, she said I was too much of a perfectionist and not to worry so much. She's either very wise or I'm very transparent, my money's on the latter.

Starting to think about planning my return home too, likely this autumn. I'm enjoying my time but also starting to miss parts of home, mostly the people though. Obviously once I go home I'll start to miss Japan too. Just the way of the world. 

I'd also like to travel around a bit more at some point, and I'm realising I need to plan things properly, because acting on impulse never really happens with me. Talked to some school friends about going to tottori at some point, which should be good if we can get it arranged. 

Weather has been pretty bad of late, just loads of rain. It's dampened my motivation to get out and about as much, but the world cup being on has probably been a welcome justification for spending time indoors. 

Not that I've been locked in per se, been out and about doing the usual lunch after school type things. Other than that mostly studying to be honest. I'll try and cut back a bit during my time off though. Want to have a good amount of energy for when term starts.

Find it pretty tiring doing the daily routine at the moment, school is usually fine but I've been trying to stay switched on for longer periods of the day, something I'm not really used to. Used to get tired a lot at school too, but always thought it was just because I was a kid and growing. Maybe it was, not sure. It would be nice to have a bit of a bigger battery, but hopefully if I have a peaceful few days I'll be alright.

A few of us are staying on for next term, but apparently they might mix up the classes so we could get separated up. If I'd have known I might have thought twice about doing a second term... It's pretty tough going through the effort of making new friends again. Hopefully there will still be some familiarity though. 

Football's been going pretty good. Have a friend who's partner has started coming to play, so it's nice having a familiar face, and a bit of an audience too. Although I do find it a little distracting. They're not good spectators though cos when I scored a good goal last week they weren't watching. What's the point in doing something cool if there's no one there to see it.

On the subject, we went bowling as a group of classmates, the girls were surprisingly good and the guys were surprisingly bad. But towards the end I started throwing down some spin deliveries for fun. Most of them gutterballed but I got a strike with one, brought the ball curling back from the right. Everyone was busy chatting so no one saw. Oh well.

It was also probably the last time I'll see a few of them, which is sad. It'll be tough to see them again as they live far. Hopefully they enjoyed their time and will be back one day.

I got a bruise at football last week, don't have any idea how. It was sore then fine, but only now has it showed up as a big yellow splotch. Weird. 

Also I've been teaching random English words to my friends, nothing structured I'll usually just say something funny and they'll say it's new to them. Most recent one was "bewildered".

This much rain, in June? While it's 30+ in the UK? I'm bewildered.
