So the last week and a bit has seen a bit of a change of pace for me. I'd been mostly focussing on my studies, trying to catch up on stuff I'd missed, trying to get over this cold. But now I'm a bit less focussed on learning Japanese. I'm still doing all my homework and self study stuff, but I'm just less commited at the moment, and not doing as much extra work.

The main reason why is I've just found a few other things I'm more interested in at the moment. I've been back to the arcade a few times last week to play sound voltex. I'm getting better but still miles off the other guys. I'm up to level 8 songs now, compared to most of the rest who are around the level 11-13 area. Then there's one guy at level 17-18. I know none of this means anything to anyone but oh well. In any case, it's been pretty fun to learn and gradually improve at a meaningless endeavour. As far as I'm concerned that's what life is about.

I've also started playing pokemon champions. It's a battle sim that you can play on switch or mobile. It's really fun, I'm enjoying it so far. You just create your team using a draft system to acquire pokes, train the pokemon's stats, moves, nature and ability, then just jump into some battles against other people online. I've made it to around top 30k, no idea if that's good or bad but there's obviousy a big difference between me and the top players. For starters I don't know most pokes' abilities or movesets, which makes it all a bit of a guessing game. Do I click wide guard against volcarona because I'm guessing it's running heat wave? Am I faster than this hisuian typhlosion?

Yes and no, in case you were wondering.

It's been really fun though, I'm probably playing a bit too much at the moment, but as I'm trying to remind myself more often, I'm on holiday so I'm allowed. It also gives me something to talk about with my pokemon go playing friend.

My team at the moment is a fairly standard mega charizard one, with aerodactyl setting tailwind, garchomp and sneasler as offensive weapons, sylveon for special coverage and toxapex for the toxic infestation stall strats. It's a fun team and toxapex is my favourite so far. I was also enjoying playing with garganacl's salt cure for a while. I tried to get a mega raichu Y set to work, with no guard ability and zap cannon, but it was rubbish. Kept dying early and exposing the rest of my team. I'm also, in general, struggling to deal with rain teams with duraludon. I don't really have an answer for it, my physical attackers get walled by stamina, and sylveon dies to flash cannon. Toxapex can't set up toxic either which means you can't stall it out. Zard's weather ball is ok but then you've got to make sure you win the weather war, which isn't always easy. It's a fun challenge though, nice little puzzle to solve. I think any team you create will have some tough matchups no matter what, there's too many variables.

The other day one of my sharehouse mates gave me a rubik's cube to try and solve. I got about two thirds of the way through (the easy two thirds, obviously), before realising I can't solve it anymore.

But then I went back to my room and relearnt. I can solve the cube pretty consistently again now, but it's very slow because I learned the most basic method, compared to the slightly longer algorithms that my younger brain was armed with. 

Now that I have some time again, it would be nice to learn how to solve cubes properly, and maybe learn a bit slower and in such a way that I actually understand what I'm doing, and how to perform certain actions on the cube. I was discussing with a friend today how the cube is basically just a group under transformations, so it would be good to map certain translations to movement patterns that you can perform - then doing more complex stuff is just a matter of chaining smaller patterns together. To be honest though, I've never really been good at applying my maths...

There's a 5x5 cube in the living room that I had a go at solving. Managed to get the first layer solved but after that I've given up, every time I look at the wretched thing it fills me with dread. Thing is, I reckon it's not too different from the 3x3, just with loads of extra steps, but I just can't wrap my head around it, and it would take me way too long to intuit. I managed to solve the 2x2 though, again by applying techniques from 3x3. I was pretty happy with myself for that.

Also just remembered a fun nonsense conversation I had last week about how all sandwiches are just a different type of lasagne, and then indeed all food is actually lasagne with differing numbers of layers. The conversation started off by asking what is a pizza and what is a sandwich, and how topologically a hot dog is more of a pizza than a sandwich. Then we argued about ice cream. In the end my argument that ice cream is a pizza prevailed. To be honest I didn't really think it was up for debate but that's ok, it's good to have these open conversations without fear of judgment.

Played a couple of midweek games of chess too, I finally got a win at the third time of asking. I'm way out of my depth though. One of my housemates taught me a few basics of strategy though which has been helpful. Stuff like controlling the middle, keeping knights central, castling early. Stuff that most people probably knew ages ago. But I never really learnt strategy as a kid, just learned the rules and played. I think it helps me sometimes be a bit unpredictable though, because I'm not really following any 'best practices', so maybe for more by-the-book players I'm awkward to face. 

In reality I probably just got lucky.

In other general news, the weather has been a bit mixed, some very hot days among showery conditions, but I've avoided the worst of the rain thankfully. Been getting back to cooking a bit more, trying to spend a bit more time downstairs talking to people instead of cooping up in my room. But sometimes I do just lie in bed playing champions and watching Frasier.

I think it's given me the space to discover and rediscover some interests that I'm genuinely engaging in, as opposed to feeling obliged to do stuff. I feel like I'd like to just be doing stuff that makes me feel better and healthier during the day, rather than chasing strength so relentlessly, and with such mediocre results.

Just felt a small earthquake while writing this, just a few seconds of gentle swaying. But it's reminded me, we had an earthquake the other night. I'd finally lulled myself to sleep after being overstimulated from late night pokemon, when my phone started blaring a siren. It was an earthquake warning, and by the time I'd woken up and dismissed the notification, the earthquake started.

I just stayed in my bed the whole time, but it was strong enough that I adopted safety measures, which were to pull my duvet up a bit higher to my head just in case a shoe was shaken out of my cupboards, which sit above my bed. Luckily they held firm and it was over after 30 seconds or so. It was the strongest quake I've felt since being here, and in the morning I'd found a few small objects had been toppled and were lying on the floor. Other than that, no damage was done.

It was at 2am though which I found very inconsiderate. Some of us are trying to sleep you know.
