The last few days have seen low 20s cloudy conditions. I'm really enjoying it, even if there is the odd spot of rain. It's just nice to come home and not be soaked from sweat. 

I've discovered a new supermarket too, annoyingly my friend told me about it ages ago but I just forgot to check it out until now. It's always nice because I'm in the honeymoon period of trying all the bentos and bakery stuff. They sell quarters of pizza that are actually pretty good, we have a little toaster oven that does a good job of heating it up. I also got some chicken that was just ok, and some katsu that I haven't tried yet, but the plan is to make curry to have it with.

There also seems to be a nice selection of fruits and veg and meats. There's a different supermarket a bit further away that's good for frozen stuff and buying in larger quantities. I've got some aji fry and gyoza in the freezer for when I'm in a pinch.

My eating pattern these days is to have a small breakfast, maybe a piece of fruit with bread or yoghurt, cereal. Then I have a bigger lunch with school friends. Usually spend between 1-2k yen. Today we went to a shabu shabu place that was about 4.5k, which is nearly £30! It was worth it though in my opinion, we get as much meat as we like, and they have things like sushi, soft drinks, desserts included too. It was all good quality too. Like for example the duck sushi was much nicer than the hamazushi one. The only issue is there's a time limit which we ended up hitting, everyone else was pretty full but I could have carried on eating a bit longer.

Dinner is usually just a snack of something, maybe from conbini, I'm rarely hungry enough for anything bigger. Sometimes I'll cook something simple like rice with stir-fry, or soba.

I'm up to level 9 or so on sound voltex, feeling like I'm no longer a beginner. Only a few levels away from some of my friends so it's nice to be in touching distance. I think the difference between each level gets bigger now though. Haven't played any chess but I'm starting to improve with my rubik's cube a bit. I haven't started learning any of the top layer algorithms yet though, maybe that's a weekend task once I've finished all my chores. I'm also playing a decent amount of pokemon, trying to use a team different from the mainstream one. It's a bit more fun using different pokes but my ranking has suffered a bit as a result.

Weekend plan is to go to Koenji and Akihabara, but not sure if I'll have time for both. A friend is also hosting some sort of games event which sounds fun so I might go along to that.
