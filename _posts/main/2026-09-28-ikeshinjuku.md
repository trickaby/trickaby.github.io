First full day in Tokyo and I decided to bring out the big guns. We went to Ikebukuro first, to see the pokemon centre which is finally open after a long closure. 

The night before I forgot to charge my phone, which was annoying. I left on like 25% which would never get me through the day. My friend was also late, as he often is. I decided to get a head start and find a charger. I heard about these portable chargers you can rent from conbini and train stations etc. You scan a QR code, download an app, get the charger and pay when you're done.

Finally found one and got everything sorted. Oh also, the day before I managed to run out of data for the month, so I bought some more but it didn't kick in until around midday the next day. Which is now today. Keep up.

Anyway, no real harm done in the end. I had a phone with battery and data and had met up with my friend, so all crisis averted. Back to Ikebukuro.

We grabbed some pastries at a shop, they were French flavour. Honestly really good. Very sugary and buttery. Next was pokemon centre.

Pokemon centres are mainly just big gift shops, but they're pretty cool. They tend to have some unique attraction too. This one was a mega evo display, which was some kind of holographic sphere thingy where you could see the mega evolution animation of whichever pokemon you selected. 

Afterwards we popped into muji. This day was quite shopping heavy, which is fine but I find it tiring. Especially when I don't really want to buy much. I tried on a few shirts though, the muji shirts are cool.

It started to rain on our way to lunch, so we stopped by another game centre, this time it didn't have sound voltex. We played the yamanote line game, and a punching bag one.

We got some lunch at my favourite sushi place, which I haven't been to since mum was here. It was really good. I felt like a bit of a pro being able to ask for things like a table for two, and the bill. Also clam soup.

Next was Shinjuku. I feel like this is my home turf, so showed him around the usual stuff, like that cat advert thingy, the godzilla cinema, city hall (don't ask). After all that we stopped by the nearest taito, which was just over the road from the second nearest taito, and played some sound voltex.

We did some more shopping, at GU and muji, got some stuff, forgot an umbrella, retrieved umbrella, went to buy an eki stamp book, then went for some abura soba. I feel like I mentioned this before, but I spent ages kinda avoiding noodle dishes other than ramen, but now I'm really into soba especially. Still haven't tried udon but I'm in the mood for it. Hopefully soon.

This was another good day, we got loads done that I wanted to, and some shopping too. Also a nice long stint pushing buttons.

After dinner we headed home, bellies and bags full. It was about 8:30 when we got home, after a 9:30 start, so yeah they're pretty long days. Most days would be vaguely like this.

Salad update: about three quarters done. Not really enjoying it but maybe I can treat myself to something sweet afterwards.
