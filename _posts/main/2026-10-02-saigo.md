The author is exhausted and so was Timin at the time.

It's pretty tiring being a tour guide, constantly on alert and planning ahead. Anyway, this was our last day together in Tokyo, then my friend goes back to Osaka. We met in Akihabara, then walked around. It was pretty early so most shops were closed. We stopped off in macdonalds, just to see what it would be like. And because it was open. We had a really nice chat while we waited for the city to wake up, then went to push buttons.

Next we went to the hobby shop I wanted to go to, but was closed the last time. It was open and I got what I wanted.

Next we went to Nakano broadway, just to wander around some of the shops. It's a pretty cool place, I nearly bought a few things but in the end didn't. We stopped off in a cafe for a drink and a cake then I took him back to Akihabara to get his luggage, then to Tokyo station to take the shinkansen.

The rest of the day I spent lying down, and packing a bit for the weekend.
