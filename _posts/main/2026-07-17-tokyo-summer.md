So summer has well and truly started now. Temperatures are hitting 35, and it doesn't get much below 30 even in the evening. Honestly it's bearable, but not enjoyable. I can still go to school easily but long walks are draining. The air conditioned respite you get from a restaurant or shop is more satisfying though. It's like playing a game and you find a little checkpoint to save your progress. Maybe your character will make a fire and sleep the night. 

Yesterday morning was the England match for me. It was a 4am alarm to walk down to the living room and watch the game by myself, then for us to lose was annoying. I was a bit sleep deprived already from waking up for the other semi final, and the lack of sleep combined with the bad result made for a pretty bad mood. At school it was tough to concentrate, and I had to go home after and do a few jobs. I didn't manage to get to the gym either. 

In the evening I was still grumpy so I went for a walk. It was a really nice walk where I was exploring my surroundings a bit more, but it didn't improve my mood. I had dinner at matsuya, which I'd been meaning to try out for a while. I had the eel and beef rice bowl which was good, if a little rich. 

As I stepped back outside I thought I was going to pass out. Being full and hot isn't a nice combination, and with the lack of sleep I was a bit delerious. Still, I made it home after stopping at conbini for a smoothie. It was refreshing.

Otherwise, I'm getting a bit frustrated with school to be honest. Maybe 1 term was enough. That being said I'm learning a lot, but it just feels less fun. I don't have as many friends anymore, and I don't currently have the energy to try to make any more. 

Then randomly the 3 people I do get on with decided to move away from me halfway through class. I was initially a bit bemused but then it turned into frustration and anger. Just a lot of small things that day put me in a bad mood, and that put me over the edge.

Otherwise not much been going on. I'm trying to gradually get to know my housemates a bit more. We went to karaoke the other day which was decent. Also been on a few walks which has been nice. 

I'm looking forward to getting back on a normal sleep schedule. I think I mentioned it last time but it really messes with my mood and concentration, makes it easy for me to get sad or angry. I'm also pretty bad at being in a bad mood. I find in general these days my mood is either really good or really bad, and it's exhausting haha. Hopefully the heat doesn't make me cranky.

I'm studying a lot these days too. Hopefully not doing too much, but I think I'm improving steadily.
