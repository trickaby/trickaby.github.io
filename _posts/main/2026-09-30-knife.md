Next day was Asakusa! I haven't been back since the spring, and it's a really lovely place. The shrine itself is good, and we went early so it wasn't too busy. Neither of us are particularly spiritual, at least not at the moment. We walked around and took a few photos, then headed back down for some snacks. I had dango for the first time, mitarashi in particular was really good. They also gave us a free sample of something which was sweet but also a bit salty, as if it had soy sauce? Anyway, it tasted of dry and powder. Not bad though.

Next we grabbed some of those red bean paste cakes that are all different shapes. You know the ones? I should probably find out their names.

Ningyō-yaki. Ningyo means doll I think. Yaki just means bake. There you go Timin, now just don't forget it this time.

Next we got some melonpan, I wanted my friend to try it. We headed to a department store, I hoped we would find a spot to sit and eat. We did, on the roof! It was a cool moment of quiet in the busy city.

We headed down and then made our way to Kappabashi street, where we entered the purgatory of knife shopping.

There's nothing much interesting to say about this part, other than we spent a good hour or two walking into dozens of knife shops, for my friend to get a kitchen knife. Kappabashi street is pretty famous for it, so it's a good location.

Anyway, we didn't buy anything. I was thinking of getting one for my mum, she mentioned wanting something for fish. But knife shopping is too stressful especially when I don't really know what I want, so I'm afraid it's not gonna happen. My friend knew exactly what he wanted but didn't see anything worth pulling the trigger on. He's got some more places to go up around Osaka though so hopefully gets that sorted.

We went back to Akihabara, had lunch at hama. I've become a bit desensitised to conveyor belt sushi, especially the chain ones. But it is really cool, my friend was blown away by the whole operation. This was one of my favourite meals of our time together, although they were all good. We got the obligatory soy butter chips, and loads of sushi. Also pudding.

Next we did some shopping. I got some cool nier stuff, he got a motherboard. I had planned to go to a hobby shop but it's closed on Wednesdays. We also pushed buttons for a while.

Headed back home for a bit, then met up for a late dinner in ginza, at a gyoza place I've been meaning to go to for ages. Finally had an excuse so we went there, tried some sake too. It was a fun evening out, although it was a bit rainy. We haven't had good weather so far this autumn, just way too wet.
