Today I feel like starting from today and working my way backwards. If you read this upside down it'll be in the right order. Up to you.

Today was the last day of classes proper for this term. Rest of the days are review and test days. It was ok. There is a bit of a sombre feeling though, some people will be off home soon so it's a bit of a closing chapter. I didn't sleep well last night too so I was a bit grouchy. After school I went home, studied, took a shortish nap, studied more, then went to a concert.

I bought tickets a while ago to see Nourished by Time in Shibuya. There's a post I did about him, you can find it in the music section. I wasn't really feeling up for going to the concert, was tired and felt like I had a lot of study to catch up on. But in the end I'm really glad I went. He's a pretty small artist still, so there was no ceremony or warmup act, just straight into his set. I was really pleasantly surprised at how much I enjoyed his performance. It was very energetic, and he sings live really well. Sometimes when someone sings that low in the register, you wonder if it'll translate to the live setting, or if it'll sound a bit weak and quiet. But no, if anything it was more powerful live.

Songs and everything were really good too. He didn't play my favourite song of his, but he did all the other ones I like so no complaints here. It was just over an hour, I don't think he's built up enough of a repetoire yet to do anything longer. But to be honest it was a good length, my back was starting to hurt from standing anyway. I got chicken nuggets from maccies on the way back. No one tell mum.

Monday was a bit of a nothing day, I again didn't sleep much and was tired at school, but a bit less so. After school I took another of those 3 hour afternoon naps which do me no favours for the evening. Basically didn't get to sleep until about 5am.

Sunday I woke up and started grinding the footy, pretty much all day spent watching football and studying Japanese in between. Then I went to football which was good fun. For some reason my team was playing like the Japanese or Korean national teams, just nonstop running and frenetic pace. I was exhausted after every game. It was fun though. We scored a nice goal where a long passing sequence ended in me crossing from the right for my teammate to header in. At footy back home there's a below head height rule so we never get headed goals. They're really satisfying though. It was a really good ball too, if I do say so myself.

In my last game, I spotted someone from class sitting watching. I'd told her about football so it wasn't a surprise, turns out her boyfriend had signed up for the session after me, because he didn't get a spot in my one. Anyway, while he was playing we had a bit of a chat, so I didn't get back home until pretty late. Didn't sleep enough so I was tired for Monday. Maybe I should have gone home a bit earlier, but I quite like just having a relaxed chat to be honest. My friend talks a lot too, so it's pretty easy to just sit and listen and respond. I'm not that good at generating conversation myself so I quite like a talkative person to chat with.

Saturday didn't do much. Loads of jobs mainly and watching football. Because end of term is drawing near, all our homework deadlines are coming, so I had to do a bit of that. Oh actually in the evening I went for pizza in Ikebukuro, walked there and back which was pretty nice.

Last week in general was ok. To be honest I don't really remember much of it, but I don't think it was very eventful. Mainly just going to school, having something for lunch, then going home to study.

I know it sounds like I'm doing loads of study so my Japanese should be really good, but it really doesn't feel like it. It's probably just a case of my self-expectations rising faster than my language ability, but I have a constant feeling that I'm getting worse at understanding things. Probably best not to dwell on it too much.

Basketball playoffs are finished now, knicks won. To be honest I ran out of steam a bit after the conference finals. Once the thunder got eliminated I wasn't really bothered about who won. 

World cup has also started obviously. The timings are a bit naff for me, all in the middle of the night through to mid morning, when I'm at school. I could catch some games when I wake, but honestly I think it's better for me to do exercise and study instead. When I come home I'm too tired usually. It's also just nicer to have it out of the way.

Whenever I go out in the evening I really enjoy the atmosphere of Tokyo. But the vast majority of the time, I'm at home getting ready for bed. Maybe I should make more of an effort to get out and about. I guess I can in the summer, might be easier with more people around me too. I think I should look out for more concerts too, I really enjoyed this one and it was pretty unstressful. Knower were doing a show in Tokyo but tickets were sold out by the time I checked. That would have been great though.

Want to do more music posts soon too, been listening to a lot of music recently.
