Long time no see, eh.

Been a bit of a chaotic few weeks to be honest. I was pretty mentally tired from my school term, spent a lot of time sleeping, playing pokemon and chess, trying to keep up with my self study, as well as seeing friends. For the first week anyway. I went out a couple of times with friends to play sound voltex (more on that later), as well as trying to spend a bit more time at home cooking again, going to the gym, things like that. It was a while ago now to be honest, so I don't remember much of what happened. I knew I had a friend coming over from London too, so I had to plan what to do when he was here. I think by the end of it I only had two or three quiet days, and I foolishly sunk them into playing way too much pokemon trying to get into master ball tier. 

But I got into master ball tier so really it wasn't so foolish. My current team is running mega pyroar and aerodactyl, although I've been tweaking it around, trying out metagross and tyranitar too. I put ice fang on aero because a lot of teams don't expect it, particularly mega salamence and garchomp teams. With sneasler's feint it's a pretty easy way to take out the other team's main threat. It's quite fun taking advantage of the meta game and finding ways to exploit teams that stick to it too rigidly. I find I struggle more with the slightly more unorthodox and "weaker" teams. Pyroar is specifically for mega golisopods and psyspam, but it's decently strong as a spread damager too. I've tried a few times to play around with sun & trick room teams, but I don't think I'm a good enough player to make it work yet.

Then on Friday I took the shinkansen to Kyoto, walked around for a bit, including to the top of the station and into some random huge temple, and a cafe for some mediocre breakfast. Kyoto wasn't the main event though, that was Nara. The place with all the deer. It was pretty cool, a bit too busy for me near the station and park entrance, but still a fun experience seeing all the deer going about their days. I had no interest in interacting with any of them, was happy just watching from afar. Some tourists had bought a stack of special deer crackers that they sell, and watching them being hounded justified my decision to refrain from such shenanigans.

I stopped in a rest stop (I think that's what it was called?) which free to enter, they have a drinks machine where I helped myself to lots of tea. The staff are friendly and come up to chat and ask what you're up to. They also have an earthquake simulator thingy which looked fun. One of the people working there told me about a festival, which was that evening. I had no idea, and had to pick up my friend from the airport for 9:30ish. I decided to skip, because it would mean wandering around town killing time, waiting for the festival to start. Then I'd have to leave early, to rush over to Kansai international.

After having lunch of soba and tempura in the local shopping street, I started wandering around town to kill time, waiting for the festival to start. I walked up to the pond where the festival was due to take place, then back down because why not. I found a random little shop down an alley, so I stopped there for a tasty ice cream in an uncomfortable chair. It was a barstool type woody affair which looks nice on instagram but isn't very desserty for sitting on. It was more of a bagel seat, or avocado on toast.

Walked around some more, sat in the station for a bit, then headed to the street for the procession. I've mentioned before, but I've sort of let the whole matsuri thing pass me by on this trip. It was nice to finally see one pass me by in the more literal sense this time.

Then walked with the parade to the pond, and found somewhere to sit with a pretty poor view. I couldn't be bothered to find a better perch, or to utilise my height as a standing spectator. I'd been walking around all day and at the 20k step mark you lose a bit of that vigour. At least I do.

The pond stuff was cool, they had some boats rowing around, playing music while actors on the boat played out a story, which had some religious significance that I didn't really understand or care about. It was really cool. I took a few bad photos (not deliberately bad, just not very good or helpful), I'll upload them at some point.

I had to leave early, to rush over to Kansai international.
