I've never really been much of an umbrella person. I didn't take a raincoat to Japan, no idea why, maybe I was under the impression I would buy one here. Maybe I still will.

Anyway, the next day had lots of rain forecast, and it was a day that I would usually just stay in. But since we were on a timer, we decided to head to the Edo Tokyo museum first. I've already been with school, but we were short on time so I didn't see everything I wanted to. In particular I wanted to understand more about the transition from Edo to Tokyo. I think my understanding of Japanese history is graudally improving, it's never been my strong suit though.

I'd bought some pastries for us both to have for breakfast, but my friend was late again so I ate most before he arrived. We shared the rest after the museum. Lesson: don't be late or you'll miss pastries.

Next we popped into lumine in Shinjuku. It felt like a pretty safe rainy day play. I think it's interesting going through a big department store, just to get a feel for what people are buying, what the trends are, what prices are like etc. We didn't really spend that long there though. It was a bit less interesting than I remember from my first time, I guess cos I'm used to it now.

Next we went to Takadanobaba, my school town. We lunched at Ootoya, my favourite teishoku place. Sometimes they spell it as teisyoku. I think either is fine. I had the seasonal chicken and aubergine set which was really good. 

Next we went to taito and pushed buttons.

That takes us to evening. I decided to let my friend take the wheel, and he suggested Shibuya. We went to uniqlo because I don't really like Shibuya and I don't know what there is to do there, and he wanted to go to uniqlo.

There's a uniqlo in the same building we just came from in Takadanobaba. It was pouring with rain. Shibuya is so busy. Umbrellas keep bumping into me. My friend didn't put enough money on his card so it got declined, which was embarrassing, so I had to pay. But the girl at the checkout smiled at me which stopped the Shibuya trip from being a complete failure.

We went home after that. Slightly shorter day because the rain drains my energy.

Jumping back to the present, I've finished my salad.
