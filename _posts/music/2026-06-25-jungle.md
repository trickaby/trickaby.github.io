Let's go back to 2024. On university challenge there was a music question. The team answerer drumm n bass but the answer was jungle.

[Result](https://youtu.be/Ke_9ethHTG4?si=xURWqAvXasM2nrdJ).

Anyway it's become a bit of a meme. I do quite like jungle music and drum n bass. It's pretty simple though to be honest, most of it sounds the same to me. Or at least none of it sounds appreciably better than the rest. There's a YouTube channel called Orion that mass produces liquid drum n bass music, with some fun ps1 era covers. They scratch the itch when I have it. It's the drums I like specifically, sorry bass.

Anyway, this post is about jungle. The group? Band? Artist? I'm not really sure. I think it's 2 people plus maybe a singer sometimes? Anyway, they're good. It's also some current music that's fairly mainstream. They were even on jools Holland one new years. They also have the best music videos imo. [Example](https://youtu.be/fwq5sT-zLLk?si=52tF1LVVOc0NoFjj).

Their music in general is dance music I suppose. At least, that's what the videos suggest. They're also fifa famous which bumps them up a bit in my estimation.

For those that may not know, I was a fifa child. We'd buy the latest game most years, usually once it had come down in price a bit around Christmas time. Good times. Each game would have its own soundtrack, on menus and loading screens. The jungle song in question was [Busy earnin’](https://youtu.be/BcsfftwLUf0?si=ByuWKFVj61a1vMaN). Brings me back to the little training pitch they did while you waited for the match to load. Couldn't tell you which fifa it came from though, maybe 15? Song came out Feb '14 which sounds about right. I don't play fifa anymore, it's gotten boring.

So this is another artist outside of my normal bread and butter, when I'm in the mood for something different. Pretty good for a sunny day I reckon. I'll try and put up a few more posts about others, like Fun. and Lena Raine. I should also dedicate something to fifa songs in general right? Maybe that will be next.

But yeah, check out jungle. Or Jungle. I wonder why they named it jungle.
