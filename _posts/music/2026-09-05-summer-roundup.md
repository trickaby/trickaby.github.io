Summer music is a genre that I'm trying to discover more of. There's a short Leon Bridges EP, Texas Sun, which I feel really gets the summer mood right, but I don't really have much of a catalogue of music that I think puts me in that summer mindset.

Leon Bridges recently reased Happiness Anytime, which has a similar but different feel. It's been a good summer listen.

The new Jungle album, Sunshine, has also been a good listen. I like Jungle because they stay in their lane, they have a distinctive style and keep to it. 

DOMi & JD Beck released their second album, like 6 years after their first. I gave it a listen but it didn't really do much for me. They're very 'music nerd', which can be a good thing but this one for me was just a bit too busy, I couldn't really latch on to much of it. Probably not fair to just judge it after one listen but I'm not really bothered to give it another go. Their first album was really interesting though, had a lot of good musicians featured on it too.

Also been listening to soundtracks, recently in particular pirates of the caribbean and nier replicant. The pirates music made me feel like a proper explorer, conquering the uncharted seas, as I made the same journey to school for the hundredth time.

But yeah, I don't know if there's a big catalogue of summer music somewhere out there, but I'm looking for more. No rush though, it's not summer anymore.
