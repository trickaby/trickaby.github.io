New album alert! I discovered Devon Gilfillian from a recent Cory Wong album, he's featured on [Roses fade](https://youtu.be/yili2cs3g3s?si=DKZ4M3k1k2Zj1VYB). He's not very well known yet, but I think worth a listen.

I've been listening to his new album quite a bit lately, *Time Will Tell*. I think it's pretty good! Got some nice variety in styles but also some consistency in the overall sound that I find it hard to describe. It feels like an *album* instead of a list of songs, if that makes sense. To be fair the latter is fine, if the songs are good enough. But I do prefer long listens to adopt an overall personality, which makes me more likely to listen to them in the future.

For example I really like Butterfly 3000 by King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, because I listen to it when I want something a bit psychedelic but mellow.  

Anyway back to the album. Weirdly I really like the album cover and I think it properly describes the feel of the album, like a guy riding across the desert, going through it. Maybe it's a chicken or egg thing, where the artwork could have influenced my perception of the album, before I'd decided what to think of the music itself. Anywho...

I'd say the majority of the music I listen to is fairly upbeat, lively or relaxing. A lot of this album is a little more sombre and melancholy, which is cool. It's nice to have some variety. Starting with *Time*, which is one such song. I'd say it's a sad song without being depressing.

Next straight into the more upbeat *Moonflower*. It starts off well, and I quite like the prechorus but I feel the moonflower climax of the chorus doesn't quite hit for me. Nevertheless a decent change of pace.

I really like the song that come next, *Hold on (Hourglass)*. Feels *very* western, but in a modern way. It's fun. It feels so much like it's from a cowboy movie that I wouldn't be surprised if it's a cover, or if it just sounds like one specific song. For now I'll just be impressed. The guitar playing is really good too.

Ok I got curious, so I it up on YouTube. The description is well worth a read! [どうぞ](https://youtu.be/M7_M2svI0q0?si=z6fuBH3OHyTvoLBl). Also the music video is pretty much exactly what I pictured haha. He said he pictured the song in Django Unchained, which makes loads of sense. Gilfillian is also the guitar player too, good job.

IRL is solid too, think Cory Wong plays guitar on this. A bit more groovy RnB. But very funky, especially the chorus. 

The songs overall are quite varied, but I think the instrumentation keeps fairly consistent which stops the album from being disjointed. I'm not an expert at all but that's what I'm hearing. The kit and bass are very boomy and sitting back, the guitar is usually quite biting. Obviously the singer's voice is the same too. *Glad to be here* is a good example of this, retains the piano shape from the intro *Time*, heavy thick chords that progress slowly. But there's also a scratchy acoustic guitar that feels more western, which fits in with the mood established in *Hold On*. Also the brushes on the snare are comforting to me.

*Keep on Movin'* is probably one of my favourites from this album, it comes at just the right time, gives some relief at the right time. Most of the songs are emotive and trying to convey a message, so it's nice to have a song that's just an excuse to tap your feet. Kit player is playing VERY clean. Horn section is always welcome. Overall very groovy and funky, my kind of music.

The album winds down a bit after that, coming to a relaxed finish that usually makes me want to listen to it again from the beginning.

So yeah, actually was pleasantly surprised by this album. Maybe in the future other tracks will stand out to me, but this is a little snapshot of my listening to it at this point in time.

I hope this was interesting, I'm not super knowledgeable on how this stuff is made, so might be describing things wrong, but I'm trying to put what I'm hearing into words. I think this was my first full album write-up? I had fun listening while writing anyway. Makes me slow down and listen and think a bit more.
