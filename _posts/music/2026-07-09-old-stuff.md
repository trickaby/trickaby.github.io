A lot of the music I listen to is relatively new. Things like Vulfpeck, Cory Wong, Louis Cole, all current artists. But their influences go back to artists that were actually popular in their time, so this post will be dedicated to those. To be clear, this is just music I like, not necessarily direct ancestors of the current stuff. But there's a lot of overlap. This stuff is probably going to be a lot more recognisable and familiar than most of my other posts.

First off, Stevie Wonder. Pretty easy one this, he's probably at the top of the hill for me. My favourite albums are Innervisions and Songs in the Key of Life. 

Next up, Earth, Wind & Fire. Another classic. I came across them relatively late in life, probably when I was in university. Obviously I knew September, and a couple of other songs that I didn't know were theirs. But they're really good, exactly my kind of music. I don't really have a favourite album. Maybe I Am. But I definitely have a favourite song, [Serpentine Fire](https://youtu.be/XoI1XPqXQ90?si=gy7k46RFRbOwsf91). So funky.

Next let's talk about motown records in general. There's plenty of names under this umbrella. I just like their sound in general. Did you know that James Jamerson, the basist for the motown house band, used to play with one finger? Because he came from the double bass. Back then the electric bass was pretty new so I guess they were still figuring out the best way to play. Anyway, he shows up on a lot of classics. It's a fun rabbit hole to go down. Same goes for the drummer Bernard Purdie. 

[I want you back](https://youtu.be/y2bVIBwpCTA?si=RFk8phKVuqSfDaul). You'll all know this one, but it's just insane how talented young MJ was. Jackson 5 made some really fun music too.

Early Commodores! I think underrated, they're mainly known for Lionel Richie's singing, but some of their instrumental tracks are really good. [Machine Gun](https://youtu.be/vVoMJSMgsUM?si=YEa7sWuIYjHq1t_B) and it's younger brother [Rapid Fire](https://youtu.be/ReIjS4uWMQs?si=lfQ8prZzaui44LN2). So funky. The whole machine gun album is pretty darn good, as well as the obvious hits. I also really like Nightshift as a one off song that's a bit different. It was their first hit after Lionel left the band to go solo.

Also a big fan of that wide big band sound, but I don't really listen to much of it, or have any go-to recommendations. But I'll throw out a couple of examples. One from anime - Cowboy Bebop. [Tank!](https://youtu.be/0hfOyOBHIq4?si=8To-Ihvyc-2puUKL). The other is from the Incredibles, which has a *really* good soundtrack. Here's the end credits, [incredits](https://youtu.be/voLQroAnoLI?si=kuB0wIbgh5Och8Tq)

I'll rattle off a few more, not much to say on these. 

Prince, purple rain. Mum's favourite. Marvin Gaye also very good, and Sam Cooke. Hopefully you're getting the idea by now. Jamiroquai too, although I discovered them surprisingly recently.

Listened to a lot of these as a kid:

Madness. It's all good, and a good example of an English band sounding English. I might sing one of their songs at karaoke next time. Queen, again no surprises here. Lionel Richie gets another mention, well done him. Michael Jackson too.

I get a bit of flack for not really knowing a lot of the really famous English bands. I've listened to a bit of the beetles, but loads of bands like Rolling Stones, The Clash etc I just never really listened to.

Tower of Power! Nearly forgot them, but I guess this was one of my first exposures to a proper funk band. We played a few of their songs in band which was always fun. I'll link their [What is Hip](https://youtu.be/SN8pWdZhVaM?si=4tFpQ0WpVFoIzeaP), which is one of those internet gems.

I could probably go on forever but that's probably enough for now.
