This is a minor fascination for me, finding music with funky time signatures. I'm not going out of my way to search for them, so it's nice when one comes along.

First one is a bit of a sneak, but [Star](https://youtu.be/EM2bMvmHjds?is=OkADRcJoNpfyiecT) has a bar of 5/4 in the chorus. Around 0:55 is the first one, "And smiling in the night" are the lyrics. It's pretty subtle and I'd probably never notice if I didn't play it in band as a kid. Anyway I've put it here, because really who's going to complain about some Earth, Wind & Fire?

Two door cinema club. They're saved in my brain as one of those Fifa song bands. Anyway, they have an album, Tourist History, which I like quite a lot. [Do you want it all](https://youtu.be/gVb3Z8vOPiE?is=jz-MyDZsk_QMFK6X) is in 7/4 time! Nice.

Pokemon music, this time from Black/White, which I think has some of the best music in the series. Anyway, here's the theme from [Icirrus City](https://youtu.be/_vWKJTvCDSU?is=5iNLuPs-IOQ4ru9V). It's in 6/8 time, with a few seaky 5/8 bars thrown in too! Wow what a change-up. 

Hopefully this list grows over time, maybe there's some more I've forgotten about.

There's also some more new music that's come out, so hopefully I'll find time to write some more of these.
