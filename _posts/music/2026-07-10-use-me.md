There's a really famouse song, [use me](https://youtu.be/LuzlbR5V_hc?is=UYh8hAkKH4KeZNx0), by Bill Withers. I'm sure most of you know it.

There's also this not so famous song, [use me](https://youtu.be/muC8-VDPrIc?is=rRfMCvJBGtt04vFB), by Esther Phillips.

It's the same song, but sounds completely different - I probably wouldn't realise if it wasn't pointed out to me. Anyway, the story behind the two versions is pretty interesting, I learned about it from [this video](https://youtu.be/w-xu0x5-B9g?is=HKzF9PcF5say6bHu). I don't have much to add from my own perspective. I do think it's cool though, the same song from the same genre, but sounding completely different. One was super famous and one was relatively unknown, but I like both. Makes you think, there's got to be so much music out there that's amazing but just never reaches our ears. Anyway, hope you enjoy.
